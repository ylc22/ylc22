FAVORITE PROTEIN

Green Fluorescent Protein (GFP)

GFP is my favorite because it captures what I love about biology: a physical molecular structure can turn an invisible process into something you can actually see. Its β-barrel protects the chromophore at the center, and decades of engineering turned one natural protein into an entire toolkit for modern cell biology.

Drag to rotate · scroll to zoom · right-drag to translate. Structure: GFP, PDB 1EMA.

FAVORITE CS CONCEPT

Dynamic Programming

I like dynamic programming because it turns a problem that looks exponentially messy into a structured sequence of reusable decisions. In computational biology, sequence alignment makes that idea tangible: every cell in the matrix represents the best answer to a smaller problem.
